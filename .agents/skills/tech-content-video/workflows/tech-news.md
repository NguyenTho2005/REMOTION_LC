# Workflow: Technology News Video (30–45s)

## Target Audience
Vietnamese developers, engineering managers, and tech enthusiasts.

## Execution Sequence
1. **Identify News Trigger:** Product launch, breaking update, acquisition, security vulnerability.
2. **Verify Official Sources:** Check official blog, changelog, or CVE details. Note source URL.
3. **Formulate Hook:** Emphasize the direct impact on developers or everyday users.
4. **Script Generation:** ~100–140 Vietnamese words. Use voice `quangminh` or `baotrang`.
5. **Visual Layout:**
   - Scene 1 (0–6s): `BreakingNews` headline with pulsing red/blue alert badge.
   - Scene 2 (6–20s): `NewsTimeline` highlighting what changed.
   - Scene 3 (20–35s): Code or architecture comparison showing old vs. new.
   - Scene 4 (35–45s): Summary verdict & CTA.
6. **ZeroTTS Audio:** Generate WAV, inspect duration, update scene timing.
7. **Render & QA:** Render MP4 vertical 1080x1920, verify audio loudness & subtitle sync.
